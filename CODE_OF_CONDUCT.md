# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This is a condensed adaptation.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this community to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Trolling, deliberately inflammatory comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information, such as an email or address, without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, code review) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a concern, contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile (listed under *Team* in the [README](README.md#team)); don't discuss it in a public thread. Reports are handled with discretion and the privacy of the reporter is respected.

Maintainers who don't follow or enforce this code in good faith may face consequences decided by the other project leaders.
